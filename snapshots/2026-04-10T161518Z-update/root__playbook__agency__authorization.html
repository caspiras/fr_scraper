# Why Use FedRAMP (Agency Authorization Playbook) FedRAMP is mandatory for federal agencies. Agencies must obtain and maintain a FedRAMP authorization when cloud products/services fall within the scope of FedRAMP (per OMB M-24-15). ## Why This Playbook This playbook is a reference for agencies pursuing an initial FedRAMP authorization. For reuse, see FedRAMP Reusing Authorizations for Cloud Products Quick Guide. ## What You Will Get From This Playbook - Description of each step of the process - Federal agency, CSP, and 3PAO roles and responsibilities - Best practices and considerations for working with stakeholders - FedRAMP resources and templates
