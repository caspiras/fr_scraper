# Continuous Monitoring (Agency Playbook) OMB M-24-15 mandated changes to ConMon are being developed via Balance Improvement Releases (optional for providers currently). CSPs must maintain the system throughout Authorization phase (ConMon activities evaluated during assessment). After FedRAMP Authorized designation: CSP continuously monitors security posture and provides agencies information for ongoing authorization decisions. ## Collaborative ConMon CSPs with more than one federal agency customer are required to implement Collaborative ConMon. Described in FedRAMP Continuous Monitoring Playbook. ## ConMon Best Practices - Authorization Planning: Discuss ConMon requirements early with CSP - Continuous Monitoring: Ask CSP to hold monthly ConMon/collaborative ConMon meetings (at least one week after monthly deliverables submitted) - Monthly ConMon meeting agenda: past due POA&Ms, deviation requests, significant change requests, annual assessment status - Continuous Monitoring Accountability: Use Performance Management section of ConMon Playbook
