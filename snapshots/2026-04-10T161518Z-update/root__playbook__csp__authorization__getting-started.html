# Is FedRAMP Right For You? Federal agencies are required to obtain FedRAMP authorizations or reuse existing FedRAMP authorizations for all cloud services within the scope of FedRAMP. FedRAMP only applies to cloud services used by federal government agencies. A FedRAMP Rev5 Agency Authorization requires a security assessment based on FISMA requirements and NIST 800-53 baselines. Key business considerations: - Are you new to the federal landscape? Consider partnering with a systems integrator. - Do you already have a federal government footprint? FedRAMP authorization can be a business development driver. - FedRAMP provides cross-government visibility on the Marketplace and a single security package for government-wide reuse.
