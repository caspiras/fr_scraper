# Preparation (Agency Playbook) ## Readiness Assessment Optional but highly recommended. Moderate/High CSOs can pursue FedRAMP Ready designation. FedRAMP Ready indicates CSP has utilized a 3PAO for Readiness Assessment and is fully ready to pursue authorization. ## Pre-Authorization ### Partnership Establishment - Define mission needs and specific requirements for a CSO - Research the FedRAMP Marketplace - Evaluate CSP readiness: fully built system, mature security processes, committed leadership ### Authorization Planning - Establish recurring meetings and project leads - Identify agency team reviewers with NIST RMF knowledge - Determine review approach: Just-In-Time Linear (recommended) vs. All Deliverables Simultaneously - FedRAMP recommends Just-In-Time approach ### Work Breakdown Structure and In Process Request - Complete WBS and submit FedRAMP In-Process Request to intake@fedramp.gov - Indicates readiness for kickoff meeting coordination ### Kickoff Meeting - Purpose: formally begin FedRAMP authorization by introducing key team members and reviewing CSO - All stakeholders gain shared understanding of process, milestones, roles, and CSO security posture
