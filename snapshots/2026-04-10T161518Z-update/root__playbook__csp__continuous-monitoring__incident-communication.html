# Incident Communication Describes steps for reporting security incidents and responses to emergency directives. Stakeholders: CSPs, FedRAMP, CISA, CSP customers, interconnected systems. FISMA definition of "incident": an occurrence that actually or imminently jeopardizes the integrity, confidentiality, or availability of information or an information system; or constitutes a violation or imminent threat of violation of law, security policies, security procedures, or acceptable use policies. ## CSP General Reporting Process CSPs must report all incidents (suspected or confirmed) within one (1) hour to: - Impacted customers (via CSP Incident Information and Forms folder in FedRAMP Secure Repository) - CISA (if applicable per federal incident notification guidelines) - FedRAMP at fedramp_security@gsa.gov - Agency POCs including AOs, AO representatives, and Agency Incident Response Teams Daily updates required until recovery phase complete. Final report required after Post-Incident Activity. ## AO Responsibilities Upon receipt of CSP notification: verify CISA was notified, request daily updates and CISA tracking number, verify posting to FedRAMP secure repository, notify FedRAMP at fedramp_security@gsa.gov.
