# Significant Changes (Rev5 Playbook) Note: This section does NOT apply to CSPs who have adopted the optional Significant Change Notifications Balance Improvement Release. A "significant" change is one likely to affect the security state of the system. ## Types of Changes 1. Routine Recurring: Regular/routine maintenance, operations, vulnerability management. Does NOT require AO review/approval. 2. Transformative: Rare; alters service risk profile; requires significant new design/development/testing; extensive documentation updates. Requires AO review/approval. 3. Adaptive: Frequent; new or modified functionality; covers wide range. Requires AO review/approval. ## Significant Change Process Steps (for Transformative and Adaptive) 1. CSP and AO meet to discuss change; CSP provides Security Impact Analysis 2. If agreed to be Routine Recurring → normal ConMon; if Transformative or Adaptive → document SCR and engage assessor 3. Assessor reviews SCR and relevant security documentation 4. Assessor develops SAP documenting assessment scope 5. SCR and SAP submitted to AO for review/approval 6. CSP implements change 7. Assessor conducts testing, develops SAR, briefs CSP and AO 8. AO reviews SAR; accepts or requires remediation/rollback 9. CSP continues normal ConMon ## Scheduling with Annual Assessments Significant changes may be scheduled with annual assessment with AO buy-in. Assessment results may be reused for annual assessment under certain conditions.
