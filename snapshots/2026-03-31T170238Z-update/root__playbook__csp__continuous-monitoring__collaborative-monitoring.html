# Collaborative Continuous Monitoring (ConMon) - FedRAMP Documentation ## Overview The page documents FedRAMP's Collaborative Continuous Monitoring framework for Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) holding multiple Authority to Operate (ATO) authorizations. ## Key Purpose "For CSOs that have more than one active ATO/ATU on file with FedRAMP, CA-7 requires the CSP to implement a Collaborative ConMon approach." ## Benefits **For Agencies:** Enables shared ConMon oversight responsibility and risk-based decision-making through collaboration **For CSPs:** Creates a centralized forum for addressing ConMon activities, coordinating Deviation Requests (DRs), Significant Change Requests (SCRs), and Annual Assessments rather than managing separate agency communications ## Four-Step Implementation Framework ### Step 1: Draft Charter Development The charter must document the collaborative process and include seven sections: - Collaboration group member contacts - Meeting schedule (recommended: monthly, Tuesday-Thursday afternoons EST) - Meeting agenda - ConMon deliverables - Decision-making authorities - Agency-specific requirements - Performance management triggers ### Step 2: Inaugural Meeting Introduce members and achieve consensus on the draft charter. ### Step 3: Charter Finalization Incorporate feedback and upload the finalized charter to the secure repository. ### Step 4: Ongoing Meetings Conduct monthly recurring meetings with deliverables uploaded one week prior. ## Decision-Making Approaches **Voting Members:** Designated agency representatives hold approval authority **Comment Period:** Two-week window for group member objections; silence equals approval
