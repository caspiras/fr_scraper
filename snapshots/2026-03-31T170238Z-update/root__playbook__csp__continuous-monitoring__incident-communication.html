# Incident Communication - FedRAMP Documentation ## Overview This FedRAMP documentation page outlines procedures for reporting security incidents. The content emphasizes that "incident communications stakeholders include a variety of teams and individuals with a vested interest in the successful implementation and operations of FedRAMP," comprising Cloud Service Providers (CSPs), FedRAMP itself, CISA, federal customers, and interconnected systems. ## Key Definitions The document references FISMA's incident definition: an occurrence that jeopardizes or violates the integrity, confidentiality, or availability of information systems or data. ## Main Requirements **Reporting Timeline**: CSPs must notify affected parties within one hour of incident identification by their security teams. **Notification Recipients**: - Impacted customers via secure repository - CISA (if meeting specific attack vector criteria) - FedRAMP at fedramp_security@gsa.gov - Agency Points of Contact and Authorizing Officials **Reporting Updates**: CSPs must provide daily updates after initial notification and a comprehensive final report following completion of the incident response lifecycle's recovery phase. ## Stakeholder Responsibilities The document outlines distinct roles: - **CSPs**: Service protection, customer notification, incident reporting - **AOs**: Verification of CISA notification, documentation review, final report evaluation - **CISA**: Technical assistance and coordinated response - **FedRAMP**: Monitoring submissions and marketplace status updates ## Consequences The documentation notes that reporting failures can trigger escalation actions outlined in FedRAMP's Performance Management section.
