# Authorization Data Sharing - FedRAMP Documentation ## Overview This document outlines FedRAMP's Authorization Data Sharing (ADS) process, enabling cloud service providers to store and share authorization data on platforms meeting FedRAMP requirements. ## Key Dates & Status - **Optional Open Beta**: Begins February 2, 2026 - **Participation Requirements**: Providers must submit a sign-up form and participate in related beta programs - **Deadline**: May 22, 2026 (end of Open Beta) ## Primary Responsibilities ### Rev5-Specific Requirements **Legacy Repository Exception**: High-impact providers using self-managed legacy repositories may defer participation. **USDA Connect Mandate**: Providers must share data via USDA Connect unless using a FedRAMP-compatible trust center. **Trust Center Migration**: Providers must notify stakeholders when migrating and provide transition guidance. ### General Provider Obligations Providers must publicly share up-to-date information including: - FedRAMP Marketplace link - Service and deployment models - UEI number and contact details - Detailed service listings with security objectives - Trust center access processes and availability status **Sensitive Information Guidelines**: Providers should exclude details that "would likely enable a threat actor to gain unauthorized access" while maintaining accurate risk disclosure. **Data Retention**: Historical authorization data must remain available for three years. ## Trust Center Requirements FedRAMP-compatible trust centers must: - Provide uninterrupted access to all authorized parties - Enable programmatic data access - Maintain agency access inventories and audit logs - Support both human and machine-readable formats - Offer self-service access management features --- **Regulatory Authority**: [44 USC § 3609(a)(8)](https://fedramp.gov/docs/authority/law/) and OMB Memorandum M-24-15
