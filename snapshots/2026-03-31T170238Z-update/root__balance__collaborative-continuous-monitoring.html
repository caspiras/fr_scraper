# Collaborative Continuous Monitoring - FedRAMP Documentation ## Overview This document outlines FedRAMP's Collaborative Continuous Monitoring (CCM) process, an optional framework beginning February 2, 2026, designed to minimize burden on cloud service providers while ensuring agencies maintain security oversight. ## Key Effective Dates - **Open Beta Start:** February 2, 2026 - **Beta End:** May 22, 2026 - **Status:** Optional for Rev5 authorized providers - **Requirement:** Providers must submit sign-up forms to participate ## Core Components ### Ongoing Authorization Reports (OAR) Providers must deliver quarterly reports covering: - Authorization data changes - Planned modifications (3+ months ahead) - Accepted vulnerabilities - Transformative changes - Security recommendations Requirements include establishing feedback mechanisms, maintaining anonymized Q&A summaries, and avoiding irresponsible disclosure of sensitive information. Reports should be "spread out from the beginning, middle, or end of each quarter" to prevent agency overwhelm. ### Quarterly Reviews - **Low Impact:** Recommended synchronous reviews - **Moderate/High Impact:** Mandatory quarterly meetings open to all necessary parties - Must include registration links and target dates for next reviews - Should occur 3-10 business days after OAR release - Providers may record sessions but must remove agency-specific information before public sharing ### Agency Responsibilities Agencies must: - Review each OAR for impact on risk tolerance - Notify FedRAMP of significant concerns via info@fedramp.gov - Report additional provider information requests to FedRAMP - Avoid imposing requirements beyond FedRAMP standards without documented justification - Consider assigning senior officials for High-impact systems ## Foundational Authority The process derives from OMB Circular A-130 section 4(c) continuous monitoring requirements and the FedRAMP Authorization Act (44 USC § 3609).
