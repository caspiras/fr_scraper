# Agency Liaison Program - FedRAMP Documentation ## Overview The Agency Liaison Program establishes dedicated points of contact within federal agencies who serve as bridges connecting those agencies to FedRAMP. According to the documentation, these individuals form "a voluntary community of trained individuals that will serve as a unified voice across federal agencies." ## Key Program Benefits The initiative offers several advantages: - **Enhanced collaboration** through direct communication channels between federal agencies and FedRAMP - **Greater FedRAMP awareness** by spreading understanding throughout government - **Operational efficiency** via centralized expertise that accelerates authorizations - **Improved transparency** regarding program updates and strategic initiatives ## Role and Responsibilities Agency liaisons are equipped to address inquiries about FedRAMP processes, the reuse methodology, initial authorization procedures, and continuous monitoring (ConMon). They participate in FedRAMP meetings to stay current on initiatives and requirements, provide vendor status information within their agencies, and facilitate partnership discussions for authorizations. ## Getting Connected Most major federal agencies have designated liaisons available as subject matter experts. For agencies without an identified liaison or those interested in joining the community, the documentation directs inquiries to info@fedramp.gov. FedRAMP can assist in designating contact points and enrolling agencies in the program.
