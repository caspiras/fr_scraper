# Is FedRAMP Right For You? - Content Summary ## Main Page Content **Key Message:** Federal agencies must obtain FedRAMP authorizations for cloud services within FedRAMP's scope. Any company selling cloud services to the government that meets defined criteria requires FedRAMP authorization. ## Core Requirements The page emphasizes that "FedRAMP only applies to cloud services used by federal government agencies." The framework cannot allocate resources toward assessing services lacking a federal agency use-case. ## Authorization Standards Obtaining Rev5 Agency Authorization requires: - Security assessment based on FISMA requirements - NIST 800-53 baselines compliance ## Business Considerations The documentation advises companies to evaluate their federal market strategy: - **New entrants:** May benefit from partnering with experienced systems integrators - **Established players:** Can leverage FedRAMP as a business development tool The authorization provides "cross-government visibility" and creates "a single security package that can be leveraged by multiple federal agencies for review." ## Important Notes - The initial authorizing agency determines federal information qualification and FedRAMP applicability - Additional resources, templates, and guidance are available on FedRAMP's official website The page is part of the FedRAMP Rev5 Cloud Service Provider documentation pathway.
