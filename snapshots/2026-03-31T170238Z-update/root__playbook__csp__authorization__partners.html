# Partners in the Authorization Process - FedRAMP Documentation ## Overview The FedRAMP authorization ecosystem involves three primary partners: ### 1. FedRAMP FedRAMP serves as the central steward, responsible for "providing a unified process to stakeholders" and maintaining a secure repository of authorized packages. The organization coordinates multiple groups within the FedRAMP ecosystem. **Secure Repository Functions:** - Manages repositories on USDA Connect.gov for LI-SaaS, Low, and Moderate categorized offerings - Grants temporary 60-day access to agency representatives reviewing authorization packages - Provides permanent access once an Authorization to Operate (ATO) is issued - Facilitates access requests through the FedRAMP Package Access Request Form For High-category systems, CSPs must establish their own secure repositories in FedRAMP-authorized environments or internally controlled systems. ### 2. Federal Agencies Agencies partner with CSPs throughout the authorization process and maintain ongoing oversight responsibilities. **Key Role - Agency Authorizing Official (AO):** A senior federal official who makes risk-based decisions to grant an ATO or Authority to Use (ATU). The documentation emphasizes that "the initial federal agency ATO is not a government-wide risk acceptance," meaning each agency must independently authorize systems for its own use. ### 3. Third Party Assessment Organizations (3PAOs) Independent assessors perform initial and periodic evaluations to ensure compliance with FedRAMP requirements. FedRAMP-recognized 3PAOs meet specific quality and independence standards, though federal agencies may alternatively use their own IV&V teams with proper attestation.
