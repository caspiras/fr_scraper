# Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M) - FedRAMP Documentation ## Overview The POA&M is a required document for Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) seeking FedRAMP authorization. It documents remediation plans for risks, weaknesses, deficiencies, and vulnerabilities identified during security assessments and continuous monitoring activities. ## Key Requirements **Submission and Correspondence:** - CSPs must submit a POA&M with their initial authorization package using the official FedRAMP template - Every risk in the Security Assessment Report's Risk Exposure Table must have a corresponding POA&M item - The document tracks past due scan-related risks during continuous monitoring **Risk Categories:** The guidance addresses four special risk classifications: 1. **Risk Adjustments (RA):** Reductions in vulnerability severity based on compensating controls or mitigating factors 2. **False Positives (FP):** Vulnerabilities that don't actually exist; validated FPs move to the Closed tab 3. **Operational Requirements (OR):** Findings that cannot be remediated; High-level ORs cannot be approved 4. **Vendor Dependencies (VD):** Reliance on external vendors for patches; high-risk dependencies require mitigation to Moderate level within 30 days ## Remediation Timelines "FedRAMP requires Critical and High risks to be remediated within 30 days of discovery, Moderate risks within 90 days, and Low risks within 180 days." ## Best Practices **Do:** - Follow template instructions carefully - Remediate or mitigate all High risks before authorization - Map POA&M items to the Risk Exposure Table clearly - Provide evidence of vendor interactions **Don't:** - Delay vendor check-ins until after authorization - Classify VDs or ORs as closed items—they remain open risks requiring ongoing tracking
