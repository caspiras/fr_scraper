# Roles and Responsibilities - FedRAMP Documentation ## Overview This FedRAMP page outlines the division of labor between Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) and Third-Party Assessment Organizations (3PAOs) during authorization package development. ## Key Point "CSPs and 3PAOs should understand and agree on the division of roles and responsibilities with respect to the development of an authorization package." ## Responsibility Breakdown **System Security Plan (SSP):** - CSPs develop documentation using FedRAMP templates and validate advisor work - 3PAOs either develop SSP as advisors or validate completeness as assessors **Security Assessment Plan (SAP):** - CSPs review, approve, and sign the SAP - 3PAOs coordinate assessment scope, deliver SAP using templates, and provide penetration test plans **Security Assessment Report (SAR):** - CSPs supply artifacts and evidence; identify risks needing remediation - 3PAOs conduct assessments per guidelines and draft the SAR **Plans of Action & Milestones (POA&M):** - CSPs create and maintain POA&M, implementing monthly continuous monitoring - 3PAOs validate POA&M detail during annual assessments ## Important Constraint 3PAOs providing advisory services cannot assess the same CSO for two years afterward, per A2LA R311 requirements.
