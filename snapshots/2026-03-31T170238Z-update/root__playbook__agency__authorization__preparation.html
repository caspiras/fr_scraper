# FedRAMP Preparation Documentation Summary ## Overview This documentation page outlines the preparation phase for federal agencies pursuing FedRAMP authorization with Cloud Service Providers (CSPs). ## Key Sections ### Readiness Assessment The FedRAMP Readiness Assessment is optional but recommended. CSPs at Moderate or High impact levels can achieve "FedRAMP Ready" designation by utilizing a FedRAMP-recognized third-party assessor (3PAO) to conduct the evaluation. This assessment focuses on "technical capabilities versus the status of documentation" and validates the CSP's ability to meet federal mandates and security requirements. ### Pre-Authorization Phase **Partnership Establishment:** Agencies should: - Define mission needs and research potential providers - Understand data sensitivity using NIST FIPS 199 standards - Review the FedRAMP Marketplace for suitable solutions - If needed, assess CSP readiness considering factors like mature security processes and relevant certifications (CMMI, ISO, SOC2, etc.) **Authorization Planning:** The collaborative process involves: - Establishing primary points of contact between agency and CSP - Setting up regular communication (bi-weekly meetings recommended) - Identifying qualified reviewers with NIST Risk Management Framework knowledge - Choosing between "Just-In-Time Linear Approach" or simultaneous submission of all deliverables **Work Breakdown Structure & In-Process Request:** Agencies must complete a Work Breakdown Structure and submit an In-Process Request to FedRAMP, indicating readiness to coordinate kickoff meetings with the CSP and 3PAO. **Kickoff Meeting:** This formal meeting introduces key stakeholders and ensures alignment on the authorization process, roles, responsibilities, schedule, and security requirements.
