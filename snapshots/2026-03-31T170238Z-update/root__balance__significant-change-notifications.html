# Significant Change Notifications - FedRAMP Documentation ## Overview This FedRAMP documentation page establishes the Significant Change Notification (SCN) process, an optional framework for authorized cloud service providers to implement changes without requiring advance government approval. ## Key Implementation Details **Effective Date:** February 27, 2026 (Wide Release) **Adoption:** The process is voluntary for Rev5-authorized providers. Those switching must notify FedRAMP via sign-up form and coordinate with agency customers. ## Four Change Categories Providers must evaluate changes and categorize them as: 1. **Routine Recurring** - Regular maintenance, patching, and vulnerability fixes requiring no formal notification 2. **Adaptive** - Incremental functionality improvements requiring notification within 10 business days 3. **Transformative** - Major architectural or capability changes requiring detailed advance planning and notification at multiple stages 4. **Impact Categorization** - Changes requiring new assessment (excluded from SCN process) ## Core Provider Responsibilities Providers must: - Evaluate all significant changes for proper categorization - Maintain 12 months of auditable notification records - Provide notifications in human and machine-readable formats - Include specific required information: service ID, change description, customer impact, timeline, and business impact analysis ## Notification Timelines - **Transformative changes:** 30 business days advance notice, final plans 10 days prior, completion notice within 5 days - **Adaptive changes:** 10 business days after completion - **Routine recurring:** No notification required ## Emergency Provision Providers may execute changes during emergencies without advance notification, provided they subsequently document and notify all necessary parties.
