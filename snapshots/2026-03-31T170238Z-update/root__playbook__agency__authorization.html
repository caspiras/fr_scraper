# FedRAMP: Why Use It for Federal Agencies ## Key Takeaways FedRAMP authorization is **mandatory for federal agencies** using cloud services within its scope. According to OMB Memorandum M-24-15, agencies must "obtain and maintain a FedRAMP authorization when the cloud product or service falls within the scope." ## Primary Benefits **Cost and Time Savings**: Federal agencies can adopt innovative cloud solutions while meeting mission-critical needs efficiently. **Standardized Security Approach**: FedRAMP aligns with the Federal Information Security Modernization Act (FISMA) and National Institute for Standards and Technology (NIST) requirements, promoting a "do once, use many" methodology that prevents duplicative security assessments. **Data Protection Compliance**: The framework ensures federal data stored in cloud environments receives legally mandated protection. **Government Collaboration**: FedRAMP facilitates coordination across federal agencies and provides ongoing guidance throughout the authorization journey. ## About This Playbook The documentation serves as a reference guide for agencies pursuing initial FedRAMP authorization, offering: - Process step descriptions - Role clarifications for agencies, cloud service providers, and assessment organizations - Best practices for effective stakeholder collaboration - Available FedRAMP resources and templates For agencies reusing existing authorizations, separate guidance is available through the FedRAMP Reusing Authorizations Quick Guide.
