# The FedRAMP Marketplace - Content Summary ## Main Purpose The FedRAMP Marketplace is a searchable database of cloud service offerings (CSOs) that have received FedRAMP designation, helping federal agencies identify secure cloud solutions. ## Key Resources for Agencies Agencies can use the marketplace to: - Research CSOs across different FedRAMP statuses - Identify federal agencies already using authorized services - Find recognized third-party assessor organizations ## FedRAMP Designation Levels **FedRAMP Ready**: "A designation provided to CSOs which indicates that a 3PAO attests to a CSO's security capabilities" and demonstrates readiness for authorization. **FedRAMP In Process**: Applied to CSOs actively pursuing authorization. Agencies can contact providers directly or reach out to FedRAMP at intake@fedramp.gov for updates. **FedRAMP Authorized**: Designation for CSOs completing the full authorization process, available for government-wide reuse. ## Important Distinction Terms like "FedRAMP Compliant" or "FedRAMP Equivalent" are not official FedRAMP designations and do not represent certified authorization. ## Reusing Authorized Services Agencies can leverage security documentation from FedRAMP Authorized CSOs through FedRAMP's established reuse processes, reducing redundant assessment work.
