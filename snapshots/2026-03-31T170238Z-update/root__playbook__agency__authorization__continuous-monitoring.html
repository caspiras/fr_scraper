# FedRAMP Continuous Monitoring for Agencies ## Overview The page explains that OMB Memorandum M-24-15 mandated changes to continuous monitoring (ConMon) processes for cloud services affecting all federal agencies. FedRAMP is developing updated agency procedures to meet these requirements. These modernized processes are currently optional for Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) to encourage gradual adoption. ## Key Responsibilities **CSPs must:** - Continuously monitor their cloud system's security posture - Provide federal agencies with information for risk-based authorization decisions **Federal Agencies must:** - Review CSP ConMon activities to ensure adequate security - Evaluate monthly Plans of Action & Milestones (POA&Ms) - Approve deviation and significant change requests - Review annual assessment results The page notes that "failure to demonstrate a mature ConMon process will prevent or delay a FedRAMP Authorized designation." ## Collaborative ConMon Approach CSPs with multiple federal agency customers must implement collaborative ConMon to streamline oversight and reduce duplicative efforts while maintaining agency due diligence. ## Best Practices for Agencies - Communicate ConMon requirements early during authorization planning - Schedule monthly ConMon meetings (at least one week after deliverables submission) - Include agenda items: past-due POA&Ms, pending deviation requests, significant changes, and annual assessment status - Establish accountability mechanisms for CSP ConMon compliance The document references the *FedRAMP Continuous Monitoring Playbook* for detailed guidance.
