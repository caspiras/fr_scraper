# Minimum Assessment Scope Effective Date(s): Optional (Wide Release) beginning 2026-01-12. Rev5 Authorized providers MAY adopt this process in place of the traditional FedRAMP boundary after January 12, 2026. ## General Provider Responsibilities - MAS-CSO-IIR: Identify Information Resources (MUST identify all resources likely to handle federal customer data) - MAS-CSO-FLO: Information Flows and Security Objectives (MUST clearly identify and document) - MAS-CSO-TPR: Third-Party Information Resources (MUST address potential impact) - MAS-CSO-MDI: Metadata Inclusion (MUST include metadata) - MAS-CSO-SUP: Supplemental Information (MAY include additional non-CSO materials)
