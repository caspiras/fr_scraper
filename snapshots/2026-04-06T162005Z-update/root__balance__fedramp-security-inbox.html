# FedRAMP Security Inbox Effective Date(s): Required (Wide Release) beginning 2026-01-05. Applies to all FedRAMP Rev5 cloud services including Authorized, In Process, and FedRAMP Ready. Mandatory Adoption enforcement timeline: - After Jan 5, 2026: Emergency Test - 2026-03-01: Corrective action includes public notification of non-compliance - 2026-05-01: Corrective action includes complete removal from FedRAMP Marketplace - 2026-07-01: Corrective action includes ban on relisting for three months ## FedRAMP's Responsibilities - FSI-FRP-VRE: Verified Emails (@fedramp.gov or @gsa.gov with SPF/DKIM/DMARC) - FSI-FRP-CDS: Criticality Designators (Emergency, Emergency Test, Important) - FSI-FRP-UFS: Use FedRAMP_Security Email in Emergencies (fedramp_security@gsa.gov or fedramp_security@fedramp.gov) - FSI-FRP-PNT: Public Notice of Emergency Tests (at least 10 business days advance) - FSI-FRP-RQA: Required Actions - FSI-FRP-ERT: Elevated Reaction Timeframes (High: 12 hours; Moderate: 3pm ET 2nd business day; Low: 3pm ET 3rd business day) - FSI-FRP-COR: Explain Corrective Actions - FSI-FRP-RPM: Reaction Metrics (MAY track and publicly share) ## General Provider Responsibilities - FSI-CSO-INB: Maintain a FedRAMP Security Inbox - FSI-CSO-NOC: Notification of Changes (immediately notify FedRAMP of address changes) - FSI-CSO-TFG: Trust @fedramp.gov and @gsa.gov - FSI-CSO-RCV: Receive Email Without Disruption - FSI-CSO-CRA: Complete Required Actions within timeframe - FSI-CSO-EMR: Emergency Message Routing (to senior security official) - FSI-CSO-IMA: Important Message Actions (SHOULD complete within timeframe) - FSI-CSO-ACK: Acknowledge Receipt (SHOULD promptly acknowledge)
