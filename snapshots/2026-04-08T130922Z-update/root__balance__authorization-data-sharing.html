# Authorization Data Sharing Effective Date(s): Optional (Open Beta) beginning 2026-02-02. Rev5 Authorized providers MAY adopt beginning Feb 2, 2026 (must also participate in SCN and VDR betas). Beta ends May 22, 2026. Providers MUST notify FedRAMP of intent to participate. ## Rev5-Specific Provider Responsibilities - ADS-CSL-LRE: High providers using legacy self-managed repository MAY ignore ADS process until further notice. - ADS-CSL-UCP: Providers MUST share authorization data via USDA Connect Community Portal UNLESS using FedRAMP-compatible trust center. - ADS-CSL-TCM: Providers MUST notify all necessary parties when migrating to a trust center. - ADS-CSL-UTC: Providers SHOULD use a FedRAMP-compatible trust center. ## General Provider Responsibilities - ADS-CSO-PUB: Providers MUST publicly share up-to-date information (human-readable and machine-readable). - ADS-CSO-SVC: Providers MUST publicly share a detailed list of specific services and their security objectives. - ADS-CSO-CBF: Providers MUST use automation to ensure consistency between human-readable and machine-readable formats. - ADS-CSO-RIS: Providers MUST provide sufficient information but SHOULD NOT include sensitive details enabling threat actors. - ADS-CSO-HAD: Providers MUST make historical authorization data available for three years. ## Using a Trust Center - ADS-UTC-PGD: Providers MUST publicly provide plain-language policies and guidance. - ADS-UTC-AGA: Providers SHOULD share the authorization package with agencies upon request. - ADS-UTC-AAD: Providers MUST notify FedRAMP within 5 business days of denying an agency access request. ## FedRAMP-Compatible Trust Centers - ADS-TRC-USH: Trust centers MUST share authorization data without interruption. - ADS-TRC-PAC: Trust centers MUST provide documented programmatic access to all authorization data. - ADS-TRC-AAI: Trust centers MUST maintain an inventory and history of federal agency users with access. - ADS-TRC-ACL: Trust centers MUST log access and store summaries for at least six months. - ADS-TRC-HMR: Trust centers SHOULD make data available in both human-readable and machine-readable formats. - ADS-TRC-SSM: Trust centers SHOULD include self-service access management features. - ADS-TRC-RSP: Trust centers SHOULD deliver responsive performance.
