# The FedRAMP Marketplace Provides a searchable, sortable database of CSOs that have achieved a FedRAMP designation. ## FedRAMP Designations - FedRAMP Ready: 3PAO attests to security capabilities; RAR reviewed and deemed acceptable by FedRAMP. - FedRAMP In Process: Actively working toward FedRAMP authorization. - FedRAMP Authorized: Successfully completed FedRAMP authorization process; available for government-wide reuse. Note: Terms like "FedRAMP Compliant" or "FedRAMP Equivalent" are NOT certified by FedRAMP. URL: https://fedramp.gov/marketplace/products
