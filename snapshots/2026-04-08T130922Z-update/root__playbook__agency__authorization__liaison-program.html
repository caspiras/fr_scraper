# Agency Liaison Program FedRAMP Agency Liaisons are dedicated points of contact within federal agencies serving as the bridge between agency and FedRAMP. Voluntary community of trained individuals serving as a unified voice. ## Program Benefits - Increased collaboration via direct line of communication between federal agencies and FedRAMP - Increased awareness of FedRAMP throughout federal government - Greater efficiency by establishing single point of expertise - Improved visibility into program updates and strategic initiatives ## How to Leverage FedRAMP Agency Liaisons Can answer questions about FedRAMP, reuse process, initial authorization, and ConMon. Attend FedRAMP-hosted meetings for updates on new requirements. Can help identify security POCs and facilitate agency liaison designation. Contact: info@fedramp.gov
