# Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M) Note: This entire section does NOT apply to CSPs adopting the optional Rev5 Vulnerability Detection and Response Balance Improvement Release. CA-5 requires CSPs to develop a POA&M using FedRAMP POA&M Template. Must be submitted with initial authorization package. ## General POA&M Guidance - Remediation Requirements: Critical/High=30 days, Moderate=90 days, Low=180 days - All open risks captured on "Open" tab even if not past due - During ConMon: only past due scan-related risks required; all initial authorization package risks must be captured - Deviation types: Risk Adjustment (RA), False Positive (FP), Operational Requirement (OR), Vendor Dependency (VD) - VDs: High-risk VDs must be mitigated to Moderate within 30 days; monthly vendor check-in required ## POA&M Do's and Don'ts Do: Follow instructions tab, remediate/mitigate all High risks, map to SAR RET, provide vendor evidence Don't: Wait until authorized to check in with vendors, put VDs/ORs in "Closed" tab
