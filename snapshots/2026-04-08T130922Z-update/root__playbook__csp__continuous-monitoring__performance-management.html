# ConMon Performance Management Describes escalation triggers and actions when CSP is not meeting ConMon requirements. ## Escalation Levels 1. Detailed Finding Review (DFR): Request for root-cause assessment and remedy 2. CAP: Request for root-cause analysis and formal remediation plan 3. Suspension: Temporary suspension of ATO(s) 4. Revocation: Permanent revocation of ATO Agency AOs must notify FedRAMP (info@fedramp.gov) for Suspension or Revocation decisions and resolutions. ## Key Deficiency Triggers (examples) - Unique Vulnerability Count increase >20% or >10 from ATO baseline → DFR - Non-compliance with scanning requirements (first incident in 6 months) → DFR - Non-compliance with scanning requirements (subsequent incidents) → CAP - 5+ High vulnerabilities aged >30 days → DFR; aged >60 days → CAP - 10+ Moderate vulnerabilities aged >90 days → DFR; aged >120 days → CAP - Annual assessment SAP delivered <60 days before ATO anniversary → CAP - Annual assessment package late → CAP - Late incident notification → CAP - Late response to CISA Emergency Directives → CAP
