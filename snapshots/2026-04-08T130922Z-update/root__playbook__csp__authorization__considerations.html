# Important Considerations (CSP Authorization) Only formal path currently available to most CSPs is a Rev5 FedRAMP authorization performed by a federal agency. FedRAMP 20x pilot not expected to be open to public until FY26 Q4. ## Key Considerations - Existing/Potential Agency Partners: CSPs must establish partnership with a federal agency as first step - Impact Categorization (FIPS 199): Low, Moderate (80% of CSPs), or High impact levels - Low: LI-SaaS or Low baseline - Moderate: ~80% of CSP services - High: Law enforcement, emergency services, financial systems, health systems - Deployment Model: Government-Only Community, Public, Private, or Hybrid - IaaS vs. PaaS vs. SaaS (per NIST SP 800-145) - System Stack: CSO must be authorized according to appropriate FedRAMP baseline - Level of Effort: Varies by system complexity, impact level, current security posture - Key LOE categories: Project Management, Engineering, Documentation, Support - Common barriers: Boundary definition, vulnerability scanning gaps, EOL software, lack of FIPS 140 encryption, MFA gaps, poor documentation
