# Continuous Monitoring Overview Based on NIST SP 800-137. Goals: operational visibility, managed change control, incident response. ## Continuous Monitoring Process Areas - Operational Visibility: Security controls implementation, deliverables, and supporting evidence (monthly, annually, every 3 years, as-needed) - Change Control: Configuration management and security impact analysis; significant change process - Incident Response: Adequate response to security incidents and emergency directives ## Continuous Monitoring Roles & Responsibilities - CSP: Primary responsibility; proactively identifies/addresses vulnerabilities; provides ConMon deliverables; maintains secure repository - Agency AO: Reviews CSP ConMon activities; approves deviation requests, significant change requests, annual assessment results - FedRAMP PMO: Oversees daily operations; maintains USDA Connect.gov secure repository (LI-SaaS, Low, Moderate); ensures materials available to all agencies - CISA: Coordinates with FedRAMP PMO on BODs and Emergency Directives - Independent Assessment Organizations: Perform initial and annual assessments; FedRAMP recognized 3PAOs ## Monthly ConMon Reporting CA-5: Develop and maintain POA&M. CM-8: Provide updated inventory at least monthly. Monthly upload to secure repository: updated POA&M, inventory, raw vulnerability scan files, and reports.
