# Minimum Assessment Scope Effective Date(s): Optional (Wide Release) beginning 2026-01-12. Rev5 Authorized providers MAY adopt in place of traditional FedRAMP boundary after Jan 12, 2026. Requires following Significant Change Request process and FedRAMP notification via info@fedramp.gov. ## General Provider Responsibilities - MAS-CSO-IIR: Providers MUST identify a set of information resources (cloud service offering) including all resources likely to handle or impact federal customer data. - MAS-CSO-FLO: Providers MUST clearly identify, document, and explain information flows and security objectives for ALL information resources. - MAS-CSO-TPR: Providers MUST address potential impact from third-party information resources (if MAS-CSO-IIR applies), documenting: general usage, justification for use, mitigation measures, and compensating controls. - MAS-CSO-MDI: Providers MUST include metadata (including metadata about federal customer data) in the MAS scope (if MAS-CSO-IIR applies). - MAS-CSO-SUP: Providers MAY include additional materials about out-of-scope resources in a supplemental package.
