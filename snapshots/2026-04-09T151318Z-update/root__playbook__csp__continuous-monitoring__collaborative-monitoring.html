# Collaborative ConMon (Rev5 Playbook) Note: This section does NOT apply to CSPs who have adopted the optional Collaborative Continuous Monitoring Balance Improvement Release. Required for CSOs with more than one active ATO/ATU. Creates central forum for addressing ConMon questions and achieving consensus on DRs, SCRs, Annual Assessments. ## Step 1: Develop Collaborative ConMon Draft Charter Charter sections: Member Contact Information, Meeting Schedule (monthly 1-hour recommended; Tue/Wed/Thu early afternoon), Meeting Agenda, ConMon Deliverables, Decision-Making Authorities (Voting Members or Comment Period approach), Agency-specific ConMon requirements, ConMon Performance Management. ## Step 2: Hold Inaugural Collaborative ConMon Meeting Introduce members; achieve consensus on draft charter. ## Step 3: Finalize Collaborative ConMon Charter Upload final charter to top-level ConMon folder in secure repository. ## Step 4: Hold Monthly Recurring Meetings Upload deliverables at least one week before meeting. Follow typical agenda. Invite new agency AOs and update charter as membership changes. PMO can attend on as-needed basis.
